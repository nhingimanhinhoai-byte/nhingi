Desktop publishing workflow

NhinGi ICT

NhinGi_ICT is a desktop application that creates short trading-chart videos and, when the user authorizes it, sends those videos to the user's own social-media account for review and posting.

The workflow is designed for the account owner to publish their own chart recordings. It does not provide investment guarantees and it does not make trades on behalf of viewers.

What the app does

  • Creates short-form videos from the user's own trading chart recordings.
  • Formats videos for vertical platforms such as TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
  • Uses platform authorization only after the account owner gives permission.
  • Uploads to the user's own authorized account for review/posting.
