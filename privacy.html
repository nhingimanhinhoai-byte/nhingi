Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how NhinGi ICT handles information when the account owner uses the NhinGi_ICT desktop application to create and upload short-form videos.

2. Information the app may process

  • OAuth authorization data required by TikTok, YouTube, or another platform selected by the user.
  • Basic account identifiers returned by the platform as part of authorization.
  • Video files created by the user on the user's computer.
  • Upload status, platform response data, and local diagnostic logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the workflow.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the user's own account, prepare video files, upload content requested by the user, and report whether the upload succeeded or failed.

4. Local storage

The desktop workflow is designed to keep its configuration, generated videos, tokens, and operational logs on the user's own computer unless a platform API requires information to be transmitted for authentication or upload. Users should protect local configuration and token files from unauthorized access.

5. Sharing

NhinGi ICT does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to third-party platforms only as necessary to use the features the user explicitly authorizes. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Retention and deletion

The user controls local files and may delete generated videos, configuration files, logs, or locally stored authorization tokens. Revoking access in the relevant platform account can also prevent future API access.

7. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to minimize unnecessary data access. Users remain responsible for securing their computer, platform credentials, OAuth tokens, bot tokens, and any other secrets used by the workflow.

8. Children

The application is not intended for children and is not designed to knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or platform requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to nhingimanhinhoai@gmail.com.
